# CTF unit atlas

`units.webp` contains six original, AI-generated hand-painted miniature units.
Created with the built-in image_gen tool for the approved CTF visual direction.
Source concept: the project's micro-tactics discussion artwork, generated in this chat.
No third-party game artwork is used.

Atlas: 1536 × 1024; three equal columns and two rows. Top row red, bottom row blue;
columns heavy infantry, cavalry and archer. All figures face right. The Canvas host
mirrors facing and positions every figure by its foot anchor. The atlas has real
alpha transparency and is encoded as WebP (about 280 KiB).

Generation brief: a transparent 3×2 sprite atlas of full-body steel/leather/cloth
miniatures; heavy infantry with shield and short sword, mounted cavalry with a
complete horse, rider and spear, light archer with longbow and quiver. Red/blue
cloth and tack share the same equipment structures. No terrain, labels, health
bars, selection rings or base discs. Leave padding in every equal-sized cell.

Only presentation uses these assets. Horse size, equipment and animation do not
alter combat statistics, coordinates, collision, range or strategy rules.
